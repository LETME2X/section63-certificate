# Changelog — Section 63(4) certificate builder

All wording of the Schedule is taken from the Gazette of India, Extraordinary:
English — Part II, Section 1, No. 55, 25 December 2023, pp. 46–47;
Hindi — Part II, Section 1A (अनुभाग 1क), pp. 534–535, published 27 March 2024.

## Review cadence
The eight "Decisions this page does not make" are checked against new rulings and state rules
on the first of December, March, June and September. Last review: 10 September 2026.
Errors, newer rulings, or reports of a court accepting or rejecting a certificate produced here:
https://github.com/letme2x/section63-certificate/issues
